Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

Here are some common questions about our services, technologies, development process, and support.

What services do you provide?

+

We provide website development, mobile app development, UI/UX design, cloud solutions, backend API development, DevOps, AI automation, and cybersecurity services for startups and enterprises.

Which technologies do you use?

+

We work with React, Next.js, Node.js, Laravel, Flutter, React Native, AWS, Docker, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Tailwind CSS, and many other modern technologies.

How long does a project usually take?

+

Project timelines depend on complexity and features. Small websites may take 1–2 weeks, while large-scale applications can take several months. We always provide a clear timeline before starting.

Do you provide support after project delivery?

+

Yes, we provide ongoing maintenance, updates, bug fixing, performance optimization, and technical support after deployment.

Can you redesign or upgrade existing websites/apps?

+

Absolutely. We can modernize outdated websites and applications, improve UI/UX, optimize performance, and migrate systems to modern technologies.

How can I start a project with you?

+

You can contact us through our website, email, or phone. Share your project idea and requirements, and our team will guide you through the next steps.
